# KARNTEK: Precision Crafted

Create a completely original, state-of-the-art website experience for KARNTEK – Complete Compliance, a UK fire and building safety consultancy.

IMPORTANT: Do NOT copy, imitate or follow the conventional corporate consultancy website structure. Do NOT simply turn a traditional sitemap/brief into a series of rectangular sections and cards. I want you to think like a world-class digital product designer and creative technology studio creating a bespoke flagship website from scratch.

The only things that should strongly anchor the design are:

KARNTEK logo

KARNTEK brand colours

Professional, credible nature of the business

Fire & building safety sector

Everything else — layout, interaction, composition, visual storytelling, navigation behaviour and UI — should be creatively reimagined.

BRAND

Use the supplied KARNTEK logo prominently but elegantly.

Primary brand green:

#8DC63F

Supporting grey:

#6D6E71

Do NOT make the entire website green.

The visual language should primarily use:

White / warm off-white

Deep near-black

KARNTEK green

Supporting grey

Carefully selected photographic colour

Green should appear as a precision accent, not as a background colour everywhere.

Think of the green as an electronic signal running through the interface.
